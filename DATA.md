# Cosmic-chronometer data

`data/cosmic_chronometers_31.txt` contains 31 measurements of the expansion rate $H(z)$ with columns

```text
z    H(z)    sigma_H
```

where $H(z)$ and $\sigma_H$ are in km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$.

The table follows the Hubble-parameter compilation in:

Joseph Ryan, Sanket Doshi, and Bharat Ratra, **Constraints on dark energy dynamics and spatial curvature from Hubble parameter and baryon acoustic oscillation data**, *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 480 (2018) 759–767. DOI: 10.1093/mnras/sty1922.

That compilation attributes the individual cosmic-chronometer measurements to the observational studies listed in its data table. This repository uses only the 31 $H(z)$ measurements and does not use the BAO measurements from that paper.
