## PSO-mechanics diagnostics

The method notebook reproduces the five-particle numerical example used in the project presentation and then visualizes the actual production swarm. For the five initial trial cosmologies, the $\chi^2$ values are 88.92, 15.05, 116.90, 61.34, and 32.56. After the illustrative first update, the corresponding values are 17.67, 14.53, 24.05, 42.84, and 22.73.

The production flat-$\Lambda$CDM run reaches $\chi^2<14.51$ by approximately the first ten iterations and is numerically stable near the final minimum by roughly 50 iterations for seed 42. These iteration counts describe this deterministic demonstration and are not general convergence guarantees for PSO.
